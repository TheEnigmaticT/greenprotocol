:factory: *Builder Practice / Workflow*
Build a “claim-to-deployment” evidence packet for every GreenChemistry.ai case: (1) feedstock and process assumptions, (2) TEA with yield, throughput and separation steps, (3) LCA with explicit energy, water, land and end-of-life boundaries, (4) safety and exposure controls, (5) analytical/PAT and data-provenance records, and (6) a pilot decision gate.
