:factory: *Builder Practice / Workflow*
Adopt a “claim-to-unit-operation” evidence packet for every chemistry or climate-tech opportunity: (1) define the incumbent baseline and system boundary; (2) map feedstock, reaction, separation, recycle, waste, and energy flows; (3) identify the first risky unit operation; (4) run a bounded pilot with analytical and mass-balance closure; (5) update the LCA/techno-economics with measured data; and (6) record what result would change the decision. This directly mirrors the integrated capture review, DOE’s bench-to-pre-pilot structure, EPA’s technology-evaluation framing, and the wastewater study’s LCA-plus-experiment design.[1][2][4]
