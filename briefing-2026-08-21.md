:factory: *Builder Practice / Workflow*
Use a staged qualification ledger for every green-chemistry claim: (1) feedstock provenance and impurity profile; (2) lab and pilot process conditions; (3) analytical and cell/plant performance; (4) safety and regulatory status; (5) lifecycle boundary and energy/water assumptions; and (6) customer/offtake or audit evidence.
